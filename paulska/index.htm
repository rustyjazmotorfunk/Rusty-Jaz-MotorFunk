« Paul Ska » mêle les grooves funk qui font la signature du groupe depuis 2007 à des contretemps ska, portés par des cuivres éclatants. "Paul Ska" blends the funk grooves that have been the band's signature since 2007 with ska offbeats, driven by bright, punchy horns. Played live long before it was recorded, this instrumental single grew up in front of an audience. Entirely homemade: the band produced, mixed and mastered the track, then directed, shot and edited the music video.
